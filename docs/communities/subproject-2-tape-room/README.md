# Sub-project 2: The Tape Room (Video Feedback & Notes)

## 1. Overview
The Tape Room is a safe, peer-critique space where actors can share monologue work, audition self-tapes, or rehearsal clips to receive structured, constructive feedback before submitting or performing.

## 2. Main Idea & Core Pillars
Acting in a vacuum makes it difficult to assess whether an emotional arc lands or if technical details are distracting. The Tape Room provides:

1. **Clip Submissions**
   * Support for short video uploads (via Vercel Blob or direct embeds from unlisted YouTube/Vimeo).
   * Context fields: Character objective, scene stakes, context notes, and specific areas where the actor wants guidance.

2. **Craft-Specific Feedback Tags**
   * Reviewers tag notes with craft dimensions:
     * `[Objective & Action]` — Was the intention clear and active?
     * `[Truthfulness & Listening]` — Did the reaction feel organic?
     * `[Vocal & Physicality]` — Voice projection, physical choices, posture.
     * `[Framing & Eyeline]` — Technical self-tape mechanics.

3. **Timecoded Peer Comments**
   * Reviewers leave comments tied to specific timestamps in the playback timeline (e.g., at 0:42: *"The shift in tactic lands strong here"*).

4. **Studio Privacy & Trust**
   * Tapes are private to approved studio members.
   * Actors can set tapes to "Studio-wide" or "Mentors & Workshop Alumni only".
